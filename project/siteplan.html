  1. Site Name

    The site name is “DRC Kinshasa Seminaries & Institutes Coordination.” I selected this name because it clearly represents my role as a Seminaries and Institutes of Religion Coordinator and reflects the area under my supervision. It best describes the scope of my responsibilities and the mission of coordinating seminaries and institutes within Kinshasa.

  2. Site Purpose

    The site will serve as an official hub for a coordination of a seminaries and Institutes of Religion Coordination.

  3. Scenarios

    The following are some of the questions site visitor, the organization's target audience, may ask:

    How to enroll to Seminary?

    How to enroll at the Institute of Religion?

    What is the different classes' schedule?
